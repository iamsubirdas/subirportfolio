FAQ

Common questions

What documents are needed for ITR filing?

Usually PAN, Aadhaar, bank statements, Form 16 (if salaried) and details of other income or investments. I will confirm the exact list for your case.

Can you help if I live outside West Bengal?

Most filings and registrations are online, so you can share documents on WhatsApp or email and I can work with you remotely.
